# AI Enablement Radar

**A living, source-grounded intelligence project that translates AI and enterprise-technology changes into practical adoption, learning, and change actions.**

[![Daily Radar](https://github.com/plukedaniel90/ai-enablement-radar/actions/workflows/daily-radar.yml/badge.svg)](https://github.com/plukedaniel90/ai-enablement-radar/actions/workflows/daily-radar.yml)
[![Quality Checks](https://github.com/plukedaniel90/ai-enablement-radar/actions/workflows/quality.yml/badge.svg)](https://github.com/plukedaniel90/ai-enablement-radar/actions/workflows/quality.yml)

## Why this exists

New AI capabilities arrive faster than most organisations can absorb them. Release notes tell us **what changed**; this project asks the harder questions:

- Who needs to work differently?
- What behaviour or capability must change?
- What adoption risk could slow value?
- What should enablement teams do next?

The radar converts official product and research updates into a transparent, searchable dataset for enablement leaders, change practitioners, instructional designers, and transformation teams.

## What the radar publishes

| Cadence | Output |
|---|---|
| Daily | Source health, new signals, relevance tags, and an auditable monitoring ledger |
| Weekly | A structured digest of the most consequential changes |
| Monthly | Trend summaries and enablement implications |
| On demand | Human field notes submitted through the repository issue form |

## Signal model

Each detected update is enriched with:

- **Domain:** AI platforms, productivity, CRM, learning, or change
- **Audience:** sellers, managers, creators, developers, or all employees
- **Enablement lens:** awareness, knowledge, skill, reinforcement, governance, or workflow
- **Adoption risk:** low, medium, or high
- **Recommended response:** the next practical enablement action
- **Evidence:** the original source URL and publication date

## Automation and authorship

The monitoring pipeline runs automatically and commits a dated record even when no material update is found. Automated outputs are explicitly identified as such. Human field notes and interpretations are separately labelled.

The project is maintained by **Luke Daniel Praneeth Kumar** as an open experiment in AI-enabled enterprise transformation and evidence-based enablement.
